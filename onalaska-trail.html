📖 HOW TO PLAY

🚗 The Journey

  • Pick a destination & difficulty, then hit the road one leg at a time.
  • Things happen on the road — deer, blizzards, strangers. Choices with ⚡ are quick skill games: tap fast!
  • Rest to heal up when your health gets low.
  • Beat the Janssens (🚙) there — the more days you win by, the more points.
  • 📅 Daily Challenge: everyone gets the same trip today. Compare scores!
  • 💾 Your trip saves itself. Close the game anytime and tap CONTINUE later.

🍽️ Food

  • 🥩 Fresh food spoils daily — eat it first.
  • 🧀 Pantry food keeps — save it for later.
  • Eating BOTH fresh and pantry food heals your party.
  • 🍽️ Rations: at any stop, tap Rations to switch between Filling, Meager and Bare Bones. Smaller meals stretch your food but wear the family down — and only Filling meals heal.
  • Run out of food entirely and you'll start to starve.

🏹 Hunting

  • Ammo is scarce — buy it, barter for it, or find it.
  • You can only carry 100 lbs of meat — extra is wasted at −2 pts/lb.
  • Right weapon matters: shotgun for birds & small game, rifle for big game, bow is silent.
  • Pick a spot: 🌾 Field Edge (turkeys, pheasants, rabbits — long shots), 🏞️ Creek Bottom (ducks, geese, deer), 🌲 Deep Woods (deer, big bucks, bears — up close).
  • 📣 CALL (or the C key) brings an animal out into the open — wait for it to stop, then shoot. Don't call 3 times in a row too fast, or everything runs away!
  • 💨 Wind: your scent drifts the way the wind blows. Animals on that side may smell you and bolt — the upwind side is safer.
  • Keep or Donate: after a big animal, KEEP the meat for your family (up to 100 lbs) or DONATE it to Hunt for the Hungry for points. Donated meat is never wasted.
  • Keyboard: arrows aim, Space fires, C calls, K keeps, D donates.
  • NEVER shoot the white deer.

🎣 Fishing

  • Cast: hold, then let go. Farther = deeper water = bigger fish. Your bait picks which fish bite.
  • Wait: little twitches are nibbles — don't strike yet! Tap when the bobber plunges under.
  • Spinnerbait: hold to reel it in slowly — fish only bite a moving lure.
  • Fight: HOLD to reel, LET GO to give line. On “RUN” or “JUMP”, let go or you'll lose it!
  • Keep or Release: keep a fish for food, or let it go for points.
  • ❄️ In winter it's ice fishing: hold to lower your jig, tap to jig it.
  • Space bar works for “hold” on a keyboard.

💰 Money

  • Shops have regional prices — shop smart.
  • Sell off surplus food for cash.
  • The mechanic takes cash OR food for repairs.
  • ⛽ Fill your gas tank at stores — run dry and it's a $45 tow.
  • Watch for good samaritans offering free help.

❤️ Health

  • Rest & medicine heal your party.
  • Injuries slow your travel down.
  • Car issues bleed you dry until fixed.
